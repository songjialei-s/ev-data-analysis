## Anomaly and Opportunity Stations

- Low-utilization optimization candidates: **Tech Park Station, South Railway Station, Airport Service Station, East Logistics Station**.
- High-load expansion candidates: **Central Plaza Station, Lakeside Community Station**.
- High-order but below-median revenue-per-pile stations: **Tech Park Station**.

## Business Recommendations

- For **Central Plaza Station, Lakeside Community Station**, review peak-hour queues and assess incremental pile capacity because both utilization and per-pile demand are relatively high.
- For **Tech Park Station, South Railway Station, Airport Service Station, East Logistics Station**, investigate location traffic, pricing, visibility, and promotion before adding equipment because demand and utilization are both below peer medians.
- For **Tech Park Station**, review charging mix and fee structure: traffic is above median but revenue efficiency per pile is below median.
- Treat these findings as directional because the repository uses synthetic sample data; validate actions with real queue, availability, and local traffic data before investment decisions.
